# Shaded icon and animated tray icon — design

Agreed with the user on 2026-11-12, from mockups drawn at real size next to the Teams and Outlook
icons of their own notification area. Mockups (HTML, open in a browser at 100% zoom):
`side-session-notes\local_810c5a28-…\tray-icon-mocks\` (`tray-icon-zero-gradient-v5.html`: the tray,
card "0+++ con la freccia 1 px più in basso"; `app-icon-gradient-v6.html`: the app icon at every size;
`tray-busy-animation-v7.html`: the animation, speed 2).

## Why

At 16 px the tray icon looked grainy next to Teams and Outlook. Redrawing it (flat pixel art, a
front view, a line glyph) was tried and turned down: the user keeps today's drawing and wants light on
it, as Microsoft's icons have. While the app works, the icon should move.

## What changes (the user's decisions)

1. **Shading everywhere**: every frame of `assets\icon.ico` (14 sizes), every PNG in `assets\icon\`,
   and the tray icons. Light comes from the top:
   - the box, every part of it (lid, both sides, tape, tape side, `>_` stamp), from **+42%** at the top
     of the box to **−35%** at its bottom;
   - the badge from **+42%** at its top to **−28%** at its bottom;
   - "+n%" mixes the color with white by n, "−n%" with black by n; 0% (the color as today) falls at
     42/77 of the box height and 42/70 of the badge height;
   - the arrow stays flat.
   Shapes, base colors, tape, `>_` and antialiasing stay as today.
2. **Arrow 1 px lower at 16 px**, where it sat 0.75 px above the badge's center.
3. **Tray states as today**: badge blue `#0078D4` (nothing to report), green `#13A10E` (updates),
   red `#E81123` (failures), yellow `#FFB900` (working); arrow white, `#1A1A1A` on yellow.
4. **While working, the tray icon is animated**: the arrow moves down inside the yellow badge, leaves
   at the bottom and comes back from the top, **one cycle per second, 13 frames**. It stops when the
   work ends. With Windows' animation effects off, the icon stays still (the first frame).
5. **Documentation**: the 1.x gets the new icon too (same `assets\icon.ico`), so its CHANGELOG says so.
   The README's logo is `assets/icon/icon-128.png` and updates by itself; its screenshots render only
   the window's content (`src\screenshots.ps1`, `RenderTargetBitmap`), which has no icon.
6. **Release v1.13.0 of 1.x**, in a separate session, after this work is merged, so it ships the new icon.

## How (my calls, accepted with the description)

- `src\icon.ps1` stays the only place the icon is drawn (ADR 0017): the shading is gradient brushes in
  the drawing, not a filter on pixels.
- The arrow moves down at 16 **and 20 px**, which share the small grid. From 24 px up it is centered.
- The tray icons are generated by `icon.ps1` too, as `.ico` files with frames 16, 20, 24 and 32 px
  (100% to 200% scale): one per still state, and one per animation frame. `TrayIcon.cs` loads them;
  `Recolor` goes, since a shaded badge cannot be recolored from its red channel (on yellow it would
  darken the light top toward the dark arrow).
- Animation frame n (0–12) moves the arrow down by n/13 of a period and draws it a period higher too,
  both clipped to the badge, so it re-enters from the top as it leaves. The period is 13 px at 16 px
  (badge 9 px + arrow 6 px, minus the edges) and scales with the badge, rounded to whole pixels.
- The tray reads its size at startup, as today; a scale change with the app open is not followed.

## Not in scope

- An icon that follows the taskbar theme.
- Following a display-scale change while the app runs.
- Any change to README text or screenshots.
